# Code of conduct

Polyphase Float is a technical collaboration. Participation requires:

- evidence-based discussion of mathematical, numerical, and performance claims;
- respectful treatment of contributors and reviewers;
- clear separation of verified results, hypotheses, and experiments;
- disclosure of conflicts, copied material, and relevant provenance;
- no harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, or deliberate disruption;
- no publication of another person's private information without permission.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that violates these rules and
may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project. Report a
conduct issue through the private security/contact route documented in
`SECURITY.md`; do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal data.
