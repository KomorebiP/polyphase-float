INDEPENDENT LOGARITHM, EXPONENTIAL, AND POWER VALIDATION

Public family
-------------
24 of 24 inventory functions implemented and exported:

log, log_ui, log2, log10, log1p, log2p1, log10p1
exp, exp2, exp10, expm1, exp2m1, exp10m1
pow, powr, pow_ui, pow_si, pow_uj, pow_sj, pown, pow_z
ui_pow_ui, ui_pow, compound_si

Numerical contract
------------------
- independent ppf_t input and output
- nearest-even, toward-zero, upward, downward, away, and faithful modes
- exact/above/below ternary result
- signed zero, infinity, and NaN
- overflow, underflow, divide-by-zero, NaN, inexact, and range flags
- input/output aliasing
- exact algebraic cases
- no MPFR arithmetic import in the production library

Differential coverage
---------------------
- structured precision matrix: 2, 3, 5, 12, 24, 53, 80, 128, and 256 bits
- differing input and output precisions
- all five deterministic ordinary rounding modes
- faithful-rounding neighbor tests
- deterministic randomized values at 8, 17, 53, and 96 bits
- narrow exponent-range overflow and underflow tests
- NaN, signed zero, infinity, negative bases, integer and noninteger exponents
- exact powers of two and ten
- exact dyadic powers including 9^(1/2) and 81^(1/4)
- exact log1p/log2p1/log10p1 identities
- scalar, GMP-integer, and compound power interfaces

Performance boundary
--------------------
The first certified interval kernels are slower than MPFR for general log,
exp, and pow. Exact integer powers are already competitive in the measured
rows. The release is a correctness and architecture milestone, not a broad
transcendental speed claim.
